About the Artist

Hello! I'm Christine Joy Macansantos, a multidisciplinary artist and Agricultural & Biosystems Engineer who enjoys blending creativity with analytical thinking.

My passion spans painting, digital illustration, graphic design, photography, videography, and data visualization. Every piece I create reflects my belief that art should communicate, inspire, and tell meaningful stories.

"Obra de Mio" is my personal creative space—a collection of works that represent my journey, imagination, and dedication to visual storytelling.
